Forced Rep

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Forced Rep handles information when you plan, record, and analyze your workouts.

Effective date and last updated: September 20, 2026

1. Who we are

Forced Rep is a personal workout planning, set logging, and strength progression application published by Perseverance Software. For privacy questions or data requests, contact perseverancesoftware.help@gmail.com.

2. Information stored by Forced Rep

Forced Rep is engineered with a strict local-first privacy architecture. All workout programs, exercise logs, completed reps, weights, rest timer states, progression curves, and personal training notes are stored locally on your device in secured sandbox storage. Forced Rep does not require account creation, and Perseverance Software does not operate servers that collect, store, or monitor your workout data.

3. Backup, JSON export, and Cloud Safety Mirror

You can export and restore complete JSON backups at any time. In addition, Forced Rep Pro provides an optional Cloud Safety Mirror feature:

  • Zero-Knowledge Encryption: Backups mirrored to cloud destinations are encrypted client-side using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption with SHA-256 integrity verification.
  • User-Controlled Storage: Backups are saved directly into your own personal cloud accounts (such as Apple iCloud Drive or Google Drive) or local directories. Perseverance Software never operates an intermediary storage server.
  • Passphrase Privacy: Your optional encryption passphrase is never transmitted to Perseverance Software or any third party. If you forget your passphrase, encrypted backup files cannot be decrypted by anyone, including Perseverance Software.

4. Exercise technique videos and YouTube

Forced Rep allows you to view exercise technique demonstrations linked to YouTube. When you choose to play a video, network requests are made directly to YouTube's services. Google and YouTube may process device information, IP address, and cookie identifiers in accordance with their terms. For details, review the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

5. Notifications and rest timers

Forced Rep uses on-device system notifications, vibration, and audio alerts to signal when a rest interval between sets has finished. All timer calculation and notification scheduling occurs entirely on your device without communicating with external push notification servers.

6. Purchases & subscriptions

Optional purchases and Forced Rep Pro subscriptions are processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Perseverance Software never collects or stores credit card or financial account numbers. The store platforms provide receipt validation and entitlement status to unlock subscribed features on your devices.

7. Data sharing and advertising

Perseverance Software does not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your workout information. Forced Rep contains no third-party advertising SDKs and does not collect or track Apple IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) or Google Advertising ID.

8. Retention and deletion

Your training data remains solely on your device until you choose to delete it. You can purge all logs at any time using the App's clear-data options, operating system app management, or by uninstalling the application. Exported files or Cloud Mirror backups must be deleted directly from whichever storage provider you selected.

9. Security

Forced Rep relies on operating system level sandboxing and hardware-backed device encryption to safeguard local databases. You are responsible for maintaining device security (passcodes, biometrics) and keeping backup files secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this privacy policy as new features are added or regulations evolve. The updated version will always be published on this page with an updated effective date.
