Forced Rep

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Forced Rep handles information when you plan, record, and analyze your workouts.

Effective date: September 20, 2026. Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Who we are

Forced Rep is a personal workout planning, set logging, and strength progression application published by Perseverance Software. For privacy questions or data requests, contact perseverancesoftware.help@gmail.com.

2. Information stored by Forced Rep

Forced Rep stores workout programs, exercise logs, completed reps, weights, rest timer states, progression curves, and personal training notes in app storage on your device. JSON exports, Cloud Safety Mirror, and device backup services can create additional copies as described below. Forced Rep does not require account creation, and Perseverance Software does not operate servers that collect, store, or monitor your workout data.

3. Backup, JSON export, and Cloud Safety Mirror

You can export and restore JSON backups. Manual exports contain readable JSON that you choose where to save or share. For users with Pro access, Cloud Safety Mirror is on by default and automatically writes a readable JSON backup to app-specific storage unless you turn it off in the App.

  • Android: If you select another folder using the system file picker, the App also writes backup copies there. The folder may be on your device or managed by a cloud storage provider. That provider controls whether and when the files are uploaded.
  • iOS: When iCloud Drive is available, the App also writes a backup copy to its iCloud Drive container. Otherwise, the Cloud Safety Mirror copy remains on the device.
  • Encryption and access: Forced Rep does not add its own encryption or password to JSON exports or Cloud Safety Mirror files. Anyone who can access a file may be able to read its contents. Device and storage provider protections depend on your settings.
  • Other backups: Your device's backup service may separately copy app data, depending on your backup settings. Perseverance Software does not operate a backup storage server or receive these backup files.

4. Exercise technique videos and YouTube

Forced Rep allows you to view exercise technique demonstrations linked to YouTube. When you choose to play a video, network requests are made directly to YouTube's services. Google and YouTube may process device information, IP address, and cookie identifiers in accordance with their terms. For details, review the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

5. Notifications and rest timers

Forced Rep uses on-device system notifications, vibration, and audio alerts to signal when a rest interval between sets has finished. All timer calculation and notification scheduling occurs entirely on your device without communicating with external push notification servers.

6. Purchases & subscriptions

Optional purchases and Forced Rep Pro subscriptions are processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Perseverance Software never collects or stores credit card or financial account numbers. The store platforms provide receipt validation and entitlement status to unlock subscribed features on your devices.

7. Data sharing and advertising

Perseverance Software does not sell, rent, or monetize your workout information. If you save a backup with a third-party storage provider, that provider may process the file under its own policies. Forced Rep contains no third-party advertising SDKs and does not collect or track Apple IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) or Google Advertising ID.

8. Retention and deletion

You can delete workout data from the App using its clear-data options or your operating system's app management controls. Deleting app data or uninstalling the App may not delete JSON exports, Cloud Safety Mirror copies, or device backups. Remove those copies separately from their storage locations or backup services.

9. Security

App storage is protected by your device and operating system access controls according to your settings. Forced Rep does not separately encrypt JSON exports or Cloud Safety Mirror files. Keep your device, backup locations, and storage accounts secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this privacy policy as new features are added or regulations evolve. The updated version will always be published on this page with an updated effective date.
