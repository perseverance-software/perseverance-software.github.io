Privacy Policy

This policy explains how FORM handles information when you plan and record workouts.

Effective date and last updated: September 3, 2026

1. Who we are

FORM is a workout planning and training application published by Perseverance Software. For privacy questions, contact perseverancesoftware.help@gmail.com.

2. Information stored by FORM

FORM stores workout programs, exercise settings, workout history, completed sets, timer state, preferences, and related training notes locally on your device. FORM does not require an account, and Perseverance Software does not operate a server that receives this workout information.

3. Backup, import, and sharing

You can choose to export or import a JSON backup. Export happens only when you request it. If you share or save an exported file using another application or storage provider, that third party handles the file under its own privacy terms. Perseverance Software does not receive a copy.

4. Exercise videos and YouTube

FORM can play YouTube videos from links you add. When you open a video, content is requested from YouTube, and Google or YouTube may process information such as your IP address, device information, cookies, or playback activity according to their own policies. Review the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service for details.

5. Notifications and rest timers

FORM uses device notifications, sound, and vibration to alert you when a rest timer finishes. Timer state and notification scheduling are processed on your device. You can control notification permission and related settings through FORM and your device settings.

6. Purchases

If FORM offers optional purchases or subscriptions, payment is processed by Google Play or Apple. Perseverance Software does not receive your complete payment-card information. The store may provide purchase and entitlement information needed to unlock, restore, and manage paid features, subject to the store provider's privacy policy.

7. Data sharing and advertising

Perseverance Software does not sell your workout information. FORM does not contain advertising or use an advertising identifier. Information may be processed by an external service only when you deliberately use the related feature, such as YouTube playback, system sharing, or a store purchase.

8. Retention and deletion

Local FORM data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove it using FORM's clear-data option, your operating system's app-data controls, or by uninstalling the app. Files you exported must be deleted separately from every location where you chose to save or share them. Store providers may retain transaction records under their own legal and privacy obligations.

9. Security

FORM relies on the operating system's application sandbox and device security to protect locally stored information. No method of storage is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device and for choosing safe destinations when exporting a backup.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when FORM's features or legal obligations change. The latest version will remain available at this URL, and the effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
