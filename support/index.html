Contact support

Email perseverancesoftware.help@gmail.com. Please include your platform (iOS or Android), app version, device model, and a clear description of your question or issue. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive personal information.

Common questions

How do I move my workouts to a new phone?

Forced Rep stores routines and workout logs in app storage on your device, and there is no account to sign in to. Before changing devices or reinstalling, export a JSON backup or check that Cloud Backup has saved a copy you can access. Then restore that backup in Forced Rep on the new phone.

Backup files are readable, so keep them in a location you trust.

Where does Cloud Backup save my data?

With an active subscription, Cloud Backup writes a readable JSON backup to app-specific storage on your device. On Android it also writes a copy to a hidden app data folder in your Google Drive if you connect a Google account. On iPhone it writes a copy to its iCloud Drive container when iCloud Drive is available.

Perseverance Software never receives these files. See Backup, JSON export, and Cloud Backup in the privacy policy.

How do Apple Health and Health Connect work with Forced Rep?

If you turn it on in the body log, Forced Rep reads your weight from Apple Health on iOS or Health Connect on Android, so weigh-ins from a smart scale or another app appear without typing them twice. It reads nothing else and never writes to Health.

You can turn reading off in the body log at any time, or remove Forced Rep's access in the Health app (iOS) or in Health Connect settings (Android). Details are in Apple Health and Health Connect.

Why didn't my rest timer alert me?

Rest alerts are on-device system notifications with optional sound and vibration. Check that notifications are allowed for Forced Rep in your phone's settings, and that silent or focus modes are not muting them. Some Android phones also limit apps in the background to save battery; allowing Forced Rep to run in the background can help.

I bought Pro. How do I restore it on another device?

Use Restore Purchases in the app while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google Play account you used to buy it.

How do I cancel my subscription or get a refund?

Manage or cancel your subscription in your device's account settings: Apple ID Subscriptions on iOS or Google Play Subscriptions on Android. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

Refund requests go directly to Apple or Google under their refund policies. See the terms of service.

Something about an exercise looks wrong. How do I tell you?

Use Submit Report in the app. It sends the exercise, the issue category and your comment to our review inbox. Please do not include names, contact details, or medical information in the comment. See Exercise issue reports for what is sent.

How do I delete my data?

Use the app's clear-data options or your phone's app management controls. Uninstalling may not delete JSON exports, Cloud Backup copies in iCloud Drive or Google Drive, or device backups, so remove those separately. Apple Health and Health Connect keep their own records.
