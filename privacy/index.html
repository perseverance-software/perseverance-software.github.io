1. Who we are

Forced Rep is a personal workout planning, set logging, and strength progression application published by Perseverance Software. For privacy questions or data requests, contact perseverancesoftware.help@gmail.com.

2. Information stored by Forced Rep

Forced Rep stores the following in app storage on your device:

  • Your setup answers: training goal, experience, training space, equipment, training days, session length, and muscle priority.
  • Workout programs, custom exercises, exercise setup notes, and video links you add.
  • Workout history: exercises, sets, weights, reps, durations, session notes, historical copies of exercise setup notes, and techniques such as drop sets, rest-pause, and partials.
  • Optional effort ratings and joint-stress ratings (see section 3).
  • Your body log: bodyweight and body measurements you enter (see section 3).
  • Settings such as weight unit, available plates, theme, and language.
  • Review-request preferences: IDs of workouts counted toward the request threshold, pending eligibility, and the last request-attempt time. These stay in app preferences and are excluded from JSON exports and Cloud Backup; device backups may separately copy them.

Forced Rep does not require account creation. JSON exports, Cloud Backup, and device backup services can create additional copies as described in section 5. Workout logs, notes, ratings, and body log entries are not sent to Perseverance Software. Reports you choose to submit are handled as described in section 6.

Importing from Strong or Hevy: if you choose a workout export (CSV file) from Strong or Hevy, the App reads that file on your device, shows what it will import, and adds those workouts to your history only when you confirm. The file is not uploaded or kept. Before an import, the App saves a backup copy of your existing data in its private storage on your device and keeps the three most recent copies.

3. Body log and joint-stress ratings

The body log lets you record your bodyweight and four tape measurements: waist, chest, arm, and thigh. After an exercise, you can optionally rate joint stress as None, Mild, or High. These entries are health-related information that you provide. They are optional and are only recorded when you enter them.

  • Where they are kept: Body log entries and ratings are stored with your other workout data on your device. They are included in JSON exports, Cloud Backup, and device backups in the same way.
  • How they are used: The App uses them only to show your own history, trends, and analysis back to you. Joint-stress ratings are notes for your own records. They never change workout targets, and Forced Rep does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
  • Deleting them: You can edit or delete body log entries in the App. Ratings are kept with your workout history and are removed when you clear the App's data.

4. Apple Health and Health Connect

If you turn it on in the body log, Forced Rep reads your weight from Apple Health on iOS or from Health Connect on Android. Your weigh-ins from a smart scale or another app then appear in the body log without typing them twice. Reading is off until you turn it on and grant access.

  • What is read: Only weight (Body Mass in Apple Health, Weight in Health Connect), from up to the last 365 days. Forced Rep requests no other Health data types and never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Where it stays: Weigh-ins read from Health are kept in memory on your device while the App is running. They are not saved in Forced Rep's data and are not included in exports, Cloud Backup, iCloud, Google Drive, or device backups. They are never sent to Perseverance Software or anyone else.
  • Your edits: If you edit a weigh-in shown from Health, Forced Rep stores the value you typed as your own body log entry. If you delete one, Forced Rep stores only that date so the weigh-in stays hidden. Neither action changes anything in Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Limits on use: Health data is used only to show weigh-ins in your body log. It is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or data brokering, and it is not sold or shared. Forced Rep's use of information received from Health Connect follows Google Play's Health Connect policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Turning it off: You can turn reading off in the App's body log at any time. You can also remove Forced Rep's access in the Health app (iOS) or in Health Connect settings (Android).

4a. Lock-screen rest countdown

During rest, Forced Rep can show the current exercise name and remaining rest time in an iPhone Live Activity or an ongoing Android notification. This information may be visible on your lock screen and other system surfaces according to your device settings. Workout and exercise setup notes are never included. The app uses the timer saved on your device and does not upload its contents or use a push service. You control notification and Live Activity availability in system settings.

5. Backup, JSON export, and Cloud Backup

You can export and restore JSON backups. Manual exports contain readable JSON that you choose where to save or share. A backup contains your workout data, including exercise setup notes, session notes, body log entries and ratings, but never weigh-ins read from Apple Health or Health Connect. With Premium access from a subscription or Lifetime Premium, Cloud Backup is on by default and automatically writes a readable JSON backup to app-specific storage on your device unless you turn it off in the App.

  • Android and Google Drive: You can choose to connect a Google account. Forced Rep then asks for permission to store its own backup file in a hidden app data folder in your Google Drive, and to see the account's email address so the App can show which account is connected. Forced Rep cannot see or change your other Drive files. The email address and Google's account ID are stored on your device. Access tokens are managed by Google and are not stored in Forced Rep's data or backups. Disconnecting removes the account from the App and revokes Forced Rep's access, but does not delete the backup file already in Drive. You can delete it in Google Drive under Settings > Manage apps. Forced Rep's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • iOS and iCloud Drive: When iCloud Drive is available, the App also writes a backup copy to its iCloud Drive container, which you can see in the Files app. Otherwise, the Cloud Backup copy remains on the device.
  • Encryption and access: Forced Rep does not add its own encryption or password to JSON exports or Cloud Backup files. Anyone who can access a file may be able to read its contents. Device and storage provider protections depend on your settings.
  • Other backups: Your device's backup service may separately copy app data, depending on your backup settings. Perseverance Software does not operate a backup storage server and does not receive these backup files, including Google Drive and iCloud Drive copies.

6. Exercise issue reports

If you choose Submit Report, the App sends the exercise name and ID, issue category, any comment you write, app version, language, platform, time zone, a random report ID, and a random installation ID to our report service. The App saves a copy on your device first and retries if sending fails. Please do not include names, contact details, or medical information in your comment.

We use reports to review and correct exercise information. The installation ID helps enforce the limit of one report per hour and three per day. External service providers host the report service and deliver reports to a private inbox for authorized reviewers. They receive the report content, including your comment, and may process connection information such as your IP address. We do not sell reports or use them for advertising.

We normally remove delivered reports from our report database after 90 days. Reports awaiting delivery or manual resolution may remain longer. Copies held by our service providers may follow their own retention settings. Deleting the App does not remove a report already sent; contact us with the exercise name and approximate submission time if you want to request its deletion.

7. Exercise technique videos and YouTube

Forced Rep allows you to view exercise technique demonstrations linked to YouTube. When the App shows a linked video's title and thumbnail, or you choose to play a video, network requests are made directly to YouTube's services. Google and YouTube may process device information, IP address, and cookie identifiers in accordance with their terms. For details, review the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

8. Workout cards and photos

You can create an image card that summarizes a workout and share or save it. If you choose to place a card over one of your photos, the photo you pick is used on your device only to create the image. The new image is created without the original photo's metadata, such as its location. Forced Rep receives only the photo you pick and cannot browse your photo library. On iOS, saving a card asks for permission to add images to your photo library. A card is shared only when you choose a destination, and that app or service then handles it under its own policies.

9. Notifications and rest timers

Forced Rep uses on-device system notifications, vibration, and audio alerts to signal when a rest interval between sets has finished. All timer calculation and notification scheduling occurs entirely on your device without communicating with external push notification servers.

10. Purchases & subscriptions

Optional purchases and subscriptions are processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Perseverance Software never collects or stores credit card or financial account numbers. The store platforms provide receipt validation and entitlement status to unlock Premium features for subscriptions and Lifetime Premium on your devices.

Forced Rep may ask the store to show its native review prompt after five nonempty workouts saved through actual use. Imported history does not count. The app sends no workout contents with this request, records only an attempt, and waits at least 90 days before another attempt. Apple or Google decides whether the prompt appears and handles any review you submit under its own policies. Forced Rep cannot tell whether you rated it.

11. Data sharing and advertising

Perseverance Software does not sell, rent, or monetize your workout information, body log, ratings, or Health data. If you save a backup with a third-party storage provider, that provider may process the file under its own policies. Submitted exercise reports are handled by external service providers as described in section 6. Forced Rep contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not collect or track Apple IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) or Google Advertising ID.

12. Retention and deletion

You can delete workout data from the App using its clear-data options or your operating system's app management controls. Deleting app data or uninstalling the App may not delete JSON exports, Cloud Backup copies in iCloud Drive or Google Drive, or device backups. Remove those copies separately from their storage locations or backup services. Deleting Forced Rep data does not change Apple Health or Health Connect, which keep their own records. Exercise reports already submitted to the report service are subject to the retention and deletion process in section 6.

13. Security

App storage is protected by your device and operating system access controls according to your settings. Forced Rep does not separately encrypt JSON exports or Cloud Backup files. Keep your device, backup locations, and storage accounts secure.

14. Changes to this policy

We may revise this privacy policy as new features are added or regulations evolve. The updated version will always be published on this page with an updated effective date.
