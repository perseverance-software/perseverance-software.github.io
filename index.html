How it works

Made for the gym floor, not the feed.

A calm, high-contrast app you can run with one hand between heavy sets.

01

Plan

Answer a few questions about your goal, experience, schedule and equipment. Forced Rep builds an editable program around them, or you can start from one of eleven starter programs.

02

Train

Open today's workout and log each set as you go. Last session's weights and reps are already filled in, and rest timers run between sets or superset rounds.

03

Progress

Every session lands in your history and calendar. New personal records are marked the moment you set them, so you can see your lifts climb.

Free

Everything you need to train, free.

Unlimited programs, unlimited history and the full exercise library, with no logging features behind a paywall.

A program that fits you

Built from your goal, experience, training days, session length and the equipment you actually have. Edit any day or build your own from scratch.

Fast set logging

Weights and reps carry over from your last session, so most sets take a single tap. Paired supersets, drop sets, rest-pause and partials are built in, with exercise setup notes and session notes kept in history.

Rest timer

Runs between sets or superset rounds, with a lock-screen countdown and a sound or vibration alert when rest is over.

300-exercise library

Every exercise has a movement demonstration, setup cues, common mistakes to avoid and the muscles it works, with linked technique videos.

History and records

A monthly calendar of completed, unfinished and missed days, every past session in detail, and personal records with estimated one-rep maxes.

Body log

Track bodyweight and waist, chest, arm and thigh measurements. Optionally bring in weigh-ins from Apple Health or Health Connect.

Forced Rep Pro

Guidance built on your own logs.

An optional subscription for lifters who want the analysis. Everything in the free app stays free.

Progression Coach PRO

Suggests the weight and reps for each working set from your recent sessions, in steps your equipment can actually load. Effort ratings and lighter weeks are optional.

Volume Matrix PRO

A body heatmap of your weekly sets per muscle, counting direct and indirect work, compared with reference volume ranges (MEV, MAV and MRV).

Warmup & Plate Calculator PRO

Warmup sets ramped to today's working weight, and exactly which plates to load on each side using the plates your gym has.

Rep Lab PRO

Estimated 1RM to 10RM from your working sets, strength curves over time, your joint-stress notes next to load, and your bodyweight trend.

Cloud Backup PRO

Automatic readable backups, with a copy in Google Drive on Android or iCloud Drive on iPhone.

Color themes PRO

Premium themes, from deep dark suites to the warm, light Oat theme.

"Machine taken?" swaps PRO

Swap an exercise mid-workout for a curated alternative when the equipment is busy.

Privacy

Your training stays on your phone.

Forced Rep works without an account or a server of ours. Your workouts, ratings and body log are not sent to us.

Read the privacy policy

  • No accountOpen the app and start training. No sign-up and no email.
  • Stored on your devicePrograms, history and body log entries live in app storage on your phone. Export a readable JSON backup whenever you like.
  • No ads, no trackingNo third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and no advertising identifiers.
  • Health data, read onlyIf you turn it on, Forced Rep reads only your weight from Apple Health or Health Connect. It keeps weigh-ins in memory, never writes to Health and never sends them anywhere.
